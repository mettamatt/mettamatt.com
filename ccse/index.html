Questions

What is the CCSE, exactly?

The CCSE (Conocimientos Constitucionales y Socioculturales de España) is a knowledge test from Instituto Cervantes, used in the Spanish nationality application. 25 questions, 45 minutes, pass at 15 correct.

Is CCSE English official?

No. CCSE English is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instituto Cervantes or the Government of Spain. The official practice materials live on Instituto Cervantes' own site.

Do I need Spanish to use it?

You'll need some Spanish for the exam itself — it is in Spanish. CCSE English is built for English-speaking residents who can read basic Spanish but want explanations and translations to fill the gaps.

Is it free?

The full question bank is free — every question, every answer, no gating. The free tier also includes two full English explanations per day. A one-time $4.99 lifetime unlock removes the daily explanation limit and turns on deeper readiness insights and trend history. No subscription.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The question bank and explanations are bundled with the app. No account, no sign-in.
