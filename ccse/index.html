FAQ

Is CCSEasy official?

No. CCSEasy is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instituto Cervantes or the Government of Spain.

What is the CCSE exam?

CCSE stands for Conocimientos Constitucionales y Socioculturales de España. It’s a knowledge test used in the Spain nationality process.
