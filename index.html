Dominex

Strategy game · Web & iOS

Grow your kingdom. Break theirs at the seams.

Dominex is a single-player strategy game where every hex earns money and every soldier costs it. Expand your kingdom, outsmart five AI rivals, and cut their territory apart to bankrupt their armies, all while keeping your own economy alive. Inspired by the classic Slay, it brings approachable rules and deep tactical choices to your browser, iPhone, and iPad.

Play in your browser App Store playdominex.com

CCSE English

Exam prep · iPhone

Understand the CCSE in English. Remember it in Spanish.

CCSE English helps English speakers prepare for Spain’s citizenship knowledge exam with clear English explanations, spaced repetition, and realistic Spanish mock exams. It turns unfamiliar material into lasting knowledge, so learners walk into the exam feeling prepared. I built it after preparing for the CCSE myself.

App Store ccseenglish.com

Retro Game Guides

Walkthrough reader · iPhone

Old-school game guides, beautifully readable on iPhone.

Retro Game Guides turns classic game walkthroughs into a clean, searchable offline library on your iPhone. Bring the guides you already trust or follow an included original Standard Edition, with readable text, clickable contents, bookmarks, and saved progress so you can pick up exactly where you left off. No ads, accounts, or subscriptions.

App Store retrogameguides.net

Spanish Verb Master

Language learning · Web

Stop freezing on Spanish verbs.

Spanish Verb Master combines spaced repetition with instant, letter-by-letter feedback, using your hesitations and corrections to find what needs more practice. Short, focused sessions build accuracy, confidence, and lasting recall.

Start practicing spanishverbmaster.com

About

I started Lullabot in 2006 and sold it to its employees through an ESOP in 2021. Along the way, I was voted one of the best CEOs in the US in 2018.

Along the way I co-wrote Pro Drupal Development and later wrote Go Prompt Yourself!, a guide to metaprompting ChatGPT.

Iowan farm boy now living in Spain with my family. Always a programmer at heart. These are the things I’m building now, and each one brings me joy.
